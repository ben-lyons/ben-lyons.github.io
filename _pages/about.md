## Research

The research program centers on how media, politics, and science shape public understanding, democratic resilience, and health communication.

### Misinformation and News Discernment
PNAS, misinformation interventions, overconfidence, and fact-checking.

### Political Polarization and Democratic Resilience
Science megastudy, democratic norms, political violence, and Carnegie-supported research.

### Health Communication and Cancer Misinformation
Nature Aging, Human Communication Research, and Annals of Behavioral Medicine.

### Expertise and Public Understanding of Science
Current Opinion in Psychology and related work on science, expertise, and trust.
