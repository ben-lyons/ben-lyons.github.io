## Research themes

### Misinformation and News Discernment
Work on reducing misinformation, improving news discernment, and correcting overconfidence through evidence-based interventions. This research is grounded in PNAS publications and ongoing applied experiments.

### Political Polarization and Democratic Resilience
Research on political polarization, democratic norms, political violence, and the Carnegie Fellowship project, including large-scale megastudies that examine political communication and resilience.

### Health Communication and Cancer Misinformation
Studies the spread and correction of health misinformation, with work appearing in Nature Aging, Human Communication Research, and Annals of Behavioral Medicine.

### Expertise and Public Understanding of Science
Explores how expertise, trust, and science communication shape public understanding in psychology, political communication, and health discourse.
